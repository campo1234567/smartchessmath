Grand Challenge

Learn • Think • Compete • Win

Where Chess Meets Mathematics!
SmartChessMath Grand Challenge combines chess-inspired mathematics with exciting live competition. Students challenge friends, solve engaging mathematical questions, develop logical thinking, and improve their performance through friendly educational competition.

Play Grand Challenge → Back to Homepage

Live 1v1 Challenge

A player can find an opponent near their rating and begin a live match. Both players compete at the same time.

Ten Shared Questions

Both players receive the same 10 questions. Correct answers and faster responses earn points.

Challenge Friends

A player can create a friend challenge or join one. A friendly match can be played without changing the rating.

Track Your Progress

The challenge shows a player rating and scores each match from accuracy and speed. Friendly matches can leave that rating unchanged.

Educational Benefits

  • Mathematical reasoning and mental calculation
  • Logical thinking and concentration
  • Problem-solving, accuracy, and decision-making
  • Healthy competition

School Competitions

Schools can use live matches for friendly classroom competition. An organized school championship can be requested from the homepage. Tournament awards and certificates are not advertised here as a currently available Grand Challenge service.
